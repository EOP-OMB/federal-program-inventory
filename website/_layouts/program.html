What information is being displayed here?

The graph shows the spending associated with the program for each available fiscal year. Obligations are a binding agreement the government makes to spend money, either immediately or in the future, when awarding funding. Outlays are what the federal government actually spent, not just promised to be paid. See our FPI Data, Terms & Concepts page for more details.

What does the dotted line represent?

The dotted line represents an estimate of the outlays that would have maintained real per capita spending at fiscal year 2019 levels, adjusting for inflation and population growth. Inflation is measured using the Consumer Price Index for Urban Wage Earners and Clerical Workers (CPI-W) from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS). Inflation factors are computed using fiscal year fourth-quarter-over- fourth-quarter growth rates. Population is the total population of the United States, including the Armed Forces overseas and the institutionalized population from the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis’ (BEA) National Income and Product Accounts. Population growth factors are computed using fiscal year fourth-quarter-over-fourth-quarter growth rates.
