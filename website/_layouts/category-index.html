Programs and the related information listed on this page use data pulled from SAM.gov, USASpending.gov, and Treasury.gov, and other publicly available federal agency websites. For financial assistance, interest on the public debt, and acquisition programs, expenditures are obligations. For tax expenditures, expenditures are the sum of outlays and revenue losses.
