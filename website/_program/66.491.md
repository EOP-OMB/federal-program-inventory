objective: 'To assist in responding to clean water emergencies for systems that ensure
  clean water. Pursuant to the American Relief Act, 2025, Public Law (P.L.) 118-158,
  this assistance program provides grant funding for projects and activities to protect
  the health or welfare of persons affected by a clean water emergency. Eligible applicants
  include States or territories in EPA Regions 3 and 4 impacted by Hurricanes Helene
  and Milton. Funding is for projects and activities that support clean water emergencies,
  including but not limited to: (1) emergency response and preparedness; (2) water
  infrastructure repairs; (3) planning assistance for receiving other sources of funding
  to address water emergencies; (4) operations and maintenance of facilities to respond
  to emergencies; (5) establishing monitoring capabilities to respond to emergency
  situations and/or; (6) making capital and managerial improvements, as necessary,
  to return eligible facilities to conditions in which systems can feasibly sustain
  their operations in the event of an emergency. Funding priorities will be for projects
  and activities in emergency situations affecting systems that are necessary for
  preventing, limiting, or mitigating a substantial danger to public health.'
