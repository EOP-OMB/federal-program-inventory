  \ (LPWS) and a Fire Control Radar. Three variants will comprise the fully realized\
  \ system: IFPC Increment 2 Interceptor, IFPC High Energy Laser (HEL), and IFPC High\
  \ Power Microwave (HPM). \r\n\r\nMission: IFPC protects U.S. Army ground forces\
  \ and assets from aerial threats, enabling them to operate effectively on the modern\
  \ battlefield through quickly and accurately identifying a wide range of threats\
