objective: This Program was created in response to large-scale wildfires in the Western
  US. Due to lack of native seed in 2001 Congress directed the BLM to establish a
  native plant material program recommending that federal and non-federal partners
  coordinate efforts through the Plant Conservation Alliance established in 1994.
  BLM provides leadership in identifying, maintaining, and restoring Western native
  plant communities on public lands. Public lands contain a diversity of native plant
  communities comprised of over 50 ecoregions across public lands. In the future,
  BLM will continue to work with partners to focus on increasing the diversity of
  locally adapted forbs and grasses for restoration of wildlife habitat and rehabilitation
  after wildfires.
