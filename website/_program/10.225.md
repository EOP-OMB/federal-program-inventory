- description: In FY 2025, the Community Food Projects Competitive Grant Program (CFPCGP)
    awarded 13 community-driven projects, obligating a total of $2.8 million to support
    innovative efforts in food and nutrition security. These investments aimed to
    enhance food access, improve nutrition literacy, and strengthen local food system
    infrastructure across different regions. The program demonstrated strong geographic
    range, funding initiatives in municipal centers, rural communities, and tribal
    lands across the Midwest, Pacific Northwest, Southern States, and other areas.
    This broad distribution reflects CFPCGP’s commitment to regionally tailored solutions
    that meet the unique needs of various populations nationwide. The funded projects
    ranged from youth-led nutrition education and urban food distribution models to
    tribal-run food sovereignty programs. Expected outcomes include increased access
    to healthy foods in communities in need, improved nutrition knowledge among youth
    and families, strengthened local food economies, and enhanced community resilience
    through food systems. Through this round of funding, CFPCGP reinforced its mission
    to foster local capacity, increase food access, and empower communities to combat
    food insecurity effectively.
