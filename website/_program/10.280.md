- description: Agriculture remains one of the most dangerous industries in the U.S.  Producers
    are more susceptible to fatal or disabling injuries and diseases, and self-employed,
    small operators are at an even greater risk. Through a partnership between the
    Cooperative Extension Service and disability serving organizations, the AgrAbility
    connects farmers with disabilities to resources and labor-saving tools and equipment
    to prevent secondary injuries and improve overall quality of life. AgrAbility
    teams offer direct assistance and educational opportunities to help farmers return
    to or remain in agricultural production. In the past 12 months, AgrAbility staff
    coordinated on-farm assistance, assessments, assistive technology demonstrations,
    and vocational rehabilitation services for more than 1,400 farmers in 21 states.
    Project teams also offered trainings, workshops, meetings, and equipment demonstrations
    for more than 118,000 farmers, healthcare providers, Extension agents, and youth
    across the U.S.
