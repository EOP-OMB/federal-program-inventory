  to assist states in strengthening rural health care delivery systems by maintaining
  a focal point for rural health within each state. The program provides funding for
  an institutional framework that links small rural communities with state and federal
  resources to help develop long-term solutions to rural health problems. The State
  Offices of Rural Health Coordination and Development Program (SORHCDP) is a cooperative
  agreement that builds the capacity of the 50 State Offices of Rural Health and rural
  stakeholders nationwide to better coordinate and improve rural health services.
