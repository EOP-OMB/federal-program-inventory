objective: The Office of Chemical and Biological Convention Affairs (CBC) is responsible
  for overseeing U.S. implementation of the Biological Weapons Convention (BWC) and
  Chemical Weapons Convention (CWC); assessing international compliance with the BWC
  and the CWC; and developing policies to address non-compliance with these treaties
  and impede state and non-state actors from developing, producing, acquiring, possessing,
  stockpiling, retaining, transferring, and using chemical and biological weapons
  and related equipment and technologies. CBC also serves as the lead policy office
  for promoting international biosafety and biosecurity, strengthening tools and mechanisms
  like the United Nations Secretary-General’s Mechanism to investigate and attribute
  chemical and biological weapons attacks, and implementing programs relevant to the
  office’s mission.
