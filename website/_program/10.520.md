  activities. These activities include futures, options, agricultural trade options,
  crop insurance, cash forward contracting, debt reduction, production diversification,
  marketing plans and tactics, farm resources risk reduction, and other appropriate

  U.S. agricultural producers and their families, as appropriate, with the knowledge,
  skills, and tools needed to make informed risk management decisions that enhance
  profitability of their operations; and 2) deliver risk management education to farmers,
  ranchers, producers, and their families who underutilize crop insurance programs.
