objective: The purpose of the Graduate Psychology Education Program is to train doctoral
  health service psychology students, interns, and postdoctoral residents in integrated,
  interdisciplinary behavioral health. The program focuses on opioid use disorder
  (OUD) and other substance use disorder (SUD) prevention, treatment, and recovery
  services, and trauma informed care. The program prepares trainees for practice in
  community-based primary care settings in high need and high demand areas, including
  in underserved and rural communities. The program also requires development for
  health service psychology faculty.
