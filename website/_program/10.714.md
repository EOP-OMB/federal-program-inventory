objective: The Joint Fire Science Program (JFSP) aims to provide funding and science
  delivery for studies associated with managing wildland fire, fuel, and fire-impacted
  ecosystems. It was established by Congress in 1998 and is a partnership of six federal
  agencies, including the USFS, BIA, BLM, NPS, USFWS, and U.S. Geological Survey.
  The program responds to the emerging needs of managers, practitioners, and policymakers
  from local to national levels. It stresses partnerships between scientists and managers,
  with special attention to getting results to the wildland fire community. JFSP supports
  collaboration among fire researchers, managers, academic professionals, and others
  working on similar or related projects, ensuring compatibility, efficiency, and
  mutually beneficial products while reducing redundant efforts. The program also
  provides access to the latest science through publications, webinars, workshops,
  field tours, discussion forums, and other activities that promote interactions between
  managers and researchers.
