objective: "The Joint Light Tactical Vehicle (JLTV) is a joint program currently in\
  \ production for the Army and Marine Corps with procurements for the Navy and Air\
  \ Force. The JLTV replaces the High Mobility Multipurpose Wheeled Vehicle (HMMWV),\
  \ which is the current light tactical vehicle. The JLTV concept includes a 3.5-ton\
  \ Combat Tactical Vehicle and a 5.1-ton Combat Support Vehicle and is based on a\
  \ family of vehicles focused on scalable armor protection, integrated communications,\
  \ and vehicle agility and mobility required of the light tactical vehicle fleet.\
  \ The JLTV provides defensive measures to protect troops in transport, increase\
  \ payload capability, and achieve commonality of parts and components to reduce\
  \ the vehicle's overall life cycle costs. The JLTV program optimizes performance,\
  \ payload, and protection of the crew and vehicle while ensuring a design that is\
  \ transportable by CH-47, CH-53, and C-130 aircraft. \r\n\r\nMission: Provides protected,\
  \ sustained, and networked light tactical mobility to the Joint forces capable of\
  \ worldwide deployment across the full spectrum of military operations and mission\
  \ profiles under all weather and terrain conditions."
