objective: The Department of Defense (DoD), Mentor Protege Program was established
  in October 1991 to expand and enhance small business participation in DoD contracting
  opportunities and expand the technical capabilities, capacity, and overall growth
  of socio-economic small businesses.  The focus of this program is to also develop
  participants to expand, improve, broaden, and innovate the Department's Supply chain
  - focused on assisting small businesses to perform competitively for both prime
  and subcontracts as best-in-class partners in the defense industrial base.  This
  assistance listing seeks to expand participation in the program, provide developmental
  assistance that will strengthen and build the Department's supply chain, national
  security, critical technology priority areas, and partners in the DIB.  Awards fund
  the mentor's labor costs, assistance in mentoring received by Historically Black
  Colleges and Universities, Minority Serving Institutions, Apex Accelerators (formerly
  Procurement Technical Assistance Centers), Manufacturing Innovative Institutions,
  and other direct costs (on a case-by-case basis - ex. travel).
