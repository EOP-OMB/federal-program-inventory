    described in the 2014 Farm Bill, this effort is conducted across five regions

    that focus on improving rural economic development through the production of sustainable
    biomass feedstocks for biofuels and biobased products.  Between September 2020
    and 2025, the SGP funded 41 projects in 29 states of the six Sun Grant Regions
    of the United States.  Through various funded projects, the SGP supported nearly
