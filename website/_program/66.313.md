    significant registration and attendance. Future webinars on water monitoring technology
    and intelligence-led enforcement, as well as the rollout of the virtual inspector
    training course in Spanish, are planned. Additionally, they co-hosted an event

    global environmental compliance and enforcement through effective communication,
