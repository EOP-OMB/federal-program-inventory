- description: 'In FY2025, MERC supported 50 grants covering a range of topics including
    sustainable agriculture, water, public health, renewable energy, and environmental
    conservation, each involving scientists from Israel working with counterparts
    in one or more Middle East or North Africa country. The number of joint Arab-Israeli
    events and exchanges, at 111, fell short of FY2024’s total (161). In FY2025, two
    new grants were issued, while 37 closed or were terminated as part of the Foreign
    Assistance Review. No new solicitation for proposals was released in 2025.


    MERC Program grants yielded technical outcomes relevant to profitable agriculture
    and healthcare services in FY2025. Israeli, Jordanian, and Palestinian researchers
    worked together to improve the quality of olive oil allowing local oils to compete
    with European products and concretely demonstrating the economic value of cooperation
    with Israel. Their recommendations to improve the quality and stability of stored
    oil that were adopted by commercial olive oil companies. In five separate grants,
    Israeli and Palestinian scientists and medical professionals elucidated the prevalence
    and cause of rare genetic diseases, antimicrobial resistance, animal borne disease,
    and a stomach infection causing a multitude of health and developmental challenges
    in children. In each case, solutions, interventions, and drug therapies were evaluated
    and/or developed to improve the quality of life and cross-border health security.'
