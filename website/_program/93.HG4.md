  \ mapping and sequencing of individual genes. This also includes reviewing and funding\
  \ research proposals, developing training programs, coordinating international genome\
  \ research, communicating advances in genome science to the public, and reviewing\

  \ equity; and to support ethical, legal and social implications (ELSI) research\
