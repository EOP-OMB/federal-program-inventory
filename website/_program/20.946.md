  \ such that people are less dependent on personal vehicles and more likely to walk,\
  \ bike, or use transit.\n•\tTransformative approaches to understanding, predicting,\

  \ policies, and practices. \n•\tCutting-edge technologies that have the potential\

  \ sciences for transportation demand management and other policy interventions that\

  \ sector."
