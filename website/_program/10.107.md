objective: The Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) was signed into law on August 16, 2022.
  Section 22006 of this law provided additional funding to provide relief for distressed
  borrowers with certain FSA direct and guaranteed loans and expedited assistance
  to agricultural operations at risk. Section 22007 provided funding for technical
  assistance regarding farm financial management for historically underserved farmers
  and ranchers. Financially distressed borrowers require skilled technical assistance
  providers FSA is establishing a nationwide network of 1:1 TA Service Providers and
  Peer Supporters to provide knowledgeable and compassionate direct support to financially
  distressed borrowers. This funding opportunity and initiative is designed to address
  the significant financial hardships and barriers that farmers and ranchers are currently
  experiencing, including disasters and other challenges that necessitate individualized
  support. This work will provide support for organizations working with distressed
  borrowers and give them resources and training to assist farmers in accessing IRA
  authorized assistance.
