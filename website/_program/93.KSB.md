objective: To expand and improve the Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) program.
  The SBIR Program aims to increase and facilitate private sector commercialization
  of innovations derived from Federal research and development; to enhance small business
  participation in Federal research and development; and to foster and encourage participation
  of socially and economically disadvantaged small business concerns and women-owned
  small business concerns in technological innovation. To utilize the Small Business
  Technology Transfer (STTR) program. The STTR Program intends to stimulate and foster
  scientific and technological innovation through cooperative research and development
  carried out between small business concerns and research institutions; to foster
  technology transfer between small business concerns and research institutions; to
  increase private sector commercialization of innovations derived from Federal research
  and development; and to foster and encourage participation of socially and economically
  disadvantaged small business concerns and women-owned small business concerns in
  technological innovation.
