  before, during, and after pregnancy, and reduce differences in rates of infant death
  and adverse perinatal outcomes. The HS program provides grants to high-risk communities
  with infant mortality rates at least 1.5 times the U.S. national average and high
  rates of other adverse perinatal outcomes (e.g., low birthweight, preterm birth,

  the disparities in health status between the general population and individuals
