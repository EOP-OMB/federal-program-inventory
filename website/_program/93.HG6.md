  \ new technologies, and studying the impact of genomics on society.  \n\nCongress\
  \ initially established NHGRI to characterize the structure and function of the\

  \ and funding proposals to address the ethical and legal issues associated with\
  \ this research.  \n\nNHGRI participates in the NIH effort to turn discovery into\
