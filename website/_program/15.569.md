- description: The Water Education Foundation completed several 2024–2025 initiatives,
    including launching the Klamath River Basin Tour, which explored dam removal sites
    and watershed areas in California and Oregon while addressing water supply, quality,
    agriculture, tribal history, and habitat restoration through diverse speakers.
    The Foundation updated its Klamath Basin Map to reflect current rivers, lakes,
    reservoirs, farmland, urban areas, reservations, and dam removal sites, along
    with explanations of major water and habitat issues. Its main website was refreshed
    and adapted for mobile access, offering videos, story maps, news feeds, and an
    expanded multilingual format, with staff trained for ongoing maintenance. The
    Aquapedia online water encyclopedia was overhauled to provide streamlined, up-to-date,
    and vetted information on key California and western water issues, including Delta
    topics and Reclamation facilities. Additionally, the Layperson’s Guide to California
    Water was revised with current data on water history, drought and flood management,
    delivery systems, Delta challenges, water rights, and conservation options, and
    a Spanish edition was published to improve accessibility for broader audiences.
