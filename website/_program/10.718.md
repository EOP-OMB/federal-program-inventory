  The objective of Capital Maintenance and Improvement for the Forest Service is to
  ensure the longevity and efficiency of its infrastructure assets. This includes
  the maintenance and rehabilitation of mission-critical buildings, recreation sites,
  roads, bridges, dams, and the decommissioning of infrastructure assets no longer
  needed. The Forest Service aims to reduce the deferred maintenance backlog by investing
