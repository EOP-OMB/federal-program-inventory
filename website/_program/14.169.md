  housing counseling agencies that counsel and provides advice to tenants and homeowners,
  with respect to property maintenance, financial management and literacy, and other
  matters as may be appropriate to assist program clients in improving their housing
  conditions, meeting their financial needs, and fulfilling the responsibilities of

  in need of foreclosure assistance. HUD certifies every housing counselor delivering
  services at HUD-approved housing counseling agencies across the country.
