  \ – A Prevention Strategy for the United States, the Centers for Disease Control\
  \ and Prevention (CDC) called for the establishment of provider-based Emerging Infections\
  \ Sentinel Networks (EISNs) to monitor and evaluate conditions that are not covered\
  \ by health department surveillance and that are likely to be seen by specific kinds\
  \ of health providers.\nObjectives for the provider-based sentinel networks include\
  \ the following:\n-\tTo support linked groups of participating individuals or organizations\
  \ in monitoring a variety of infectious disease problems and enhancing communication\
  \ and collaboration among network members and the public health community;\n-\t\
  To serve as readily accessible mechanisms to address urgent public health infectious\
  \ disease problems rapidly; and\n-\tTo improve understanding of specific public\
  \ health issues, including, but not limited to, international travel, and enhance\
  \ preparedness to meet new infectious disease threats among domestic and globally\
  \ mobile populations.\nAdditional objectives for the \"research\" EISN include:\n\
  -\tTo focus studies on the most important issues to be addressed by participating\
  \ hospital EDs and CDC; and\n-\tTo estimate the community burden of diseases."
