objective: The Child and Adult Care Food Program (CACFP) is a federal program that
  provides reimbursements for nutritious meals and snacks to eligible children and
  adults who are enrolled for care at participating child care centers, day care homes,
  and adult day care centers. CACFP also provides reimbursements for meals served
  to children and youth participating in afterschool care programs, children residing
  in emergency shelters, and adults over the age of 60 or living with a disability
  and enrolled in day care facilities. CACFP contributes to the wellness, healthy
  growth, and development of young children and adults in the United States.
