objective: 'The Women and Minorities (WAMS) in Science, Technology, Engineering and
  Mathematics (STEM) Fields program was authorized to increase participation by women
  and underrepresented minorities from rural areas in the fields of science, technology,
  engineering and mathematics, with priority given to eligible institutions that carry
  out continuing programs funded by the Secretary.


  The goal of the WAMS program is to develop and implement robust partnerships to
  increase the representation, participation, and entrepreneurism of women in STEM
  careers, thereby contributing to national economic prosperity.


  The purpose of the grant program is to support projects that: Provide STEM knowledge,
  skills and competency to Women and Minorities from Rural Areas with successful placement
  (a) in the workforce in STEM fields, or (b) as innovators and entrepreneurs adding
  value to the STEM fields in areas that have relevancy to the USDA Secretary’s priorities.'
