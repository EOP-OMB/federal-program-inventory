objective: To further the goals of Tribal Self-Governance compacting for Tribes to
  administer a wide rage of programs with broad administrative and programmatic flexibility.   The
  Indian Self-Determination and Education Assistance Act (ISDEAA) authorizes Tribes
  and Tribal organizations to be funded by the Federal government to provide services
  that the Federal government would otherwise be obligated to provide due to the trust
  responsibilities and treaty obligations of the United States. ISDEAA establishes
  Self-Determination contracting (Title 1) and Self-Governance compacting (Title IV)
  as two unique options that Tribal governments can elect to use to assume control
  and decision-making authority over select federal programs and resources benefiting
  their citizens and communities.
