  application of fire by a team of fire experts under specified weather conditions.

  Mobilization Strategy, which was developed by a task group of incident management
  experts at the Forest Service. The strategy specifically addresses prescribed burning
  on Wildfire Crisis Strategy landscapes and across regional boundaries.
