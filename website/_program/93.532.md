  cooperative agreements. CMHS supports prevention, treatment, and recovery for Americans
  with mental health conditions. CMHS aims to improve the health and wellbeing of
  individuals, families, and communities; and for all people to live fulfilling, independent,
  and productive lives.  This is achieved through (1) knowledge development and application
  projects for prevention, treatment, and rehabilitation, and the conduct or support
