  1621 note), the overarching goals and objectives of the IR-4 program are to assist
  in the collection of residue and efficacy data in support of the registration or
  reregistration of pesticides for minor agricultural use and for use on specialty
  crops under the Federal Insecticide, Fungicide, and Rodenticide Act (7 U.S.C. 136
  et seq.) and tolerances for residues of minor use chemicals in or on raw agricultural
  commodities under sections 346a and 348 of U.S.C. Title 21. This is essential to
  solving the most pressing challenges these growers face and keeping American agriculture
  competitive and reducing hunger by improving the availability of safe and nutritious
  food.
