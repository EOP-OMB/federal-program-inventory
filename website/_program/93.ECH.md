objective: "The Environmental influences on Child Health Outcomes (ECHO) Program,\
  \ a nationwide research initiative, aims to investigate the root causes of childhood\
  \ chronic diseases and how to prevent them. The mission is to enhance the health\
  \ of children for generations to come. The Program has two major components: the\
  \ ECHO Cohort for observational research and the ECHO Institutional Development\
  \ Awards (IDeA) States Pediatric Clinical Trials Network (ISPCTN) for intervention\
  \ research. The ECHO Cohort is the largest longitudinal child health study in the\
  \ United States, with data from more than 150,000 children and their families—from\
  \ before conception through age 21 years-- integrated into one powerful resource.\
  \ The studies share standardized protocol, data elements, and biospecimen collection\
  \ procedure managed by centralized cores.  \n\nThe ECHO Institutional Development\
  \ Award (IDeA) States Pediatric Clinical Trials Network (ISPCTN) brings state-of-the-art\
  \ clinical trials to children and families in rural or underserved areas, focusing\
  \ on conditions that disproportionately affect these communities. This network also\
  \ provides capacity building and professional development to institutions in states\
  \ with historically low rates of NIH funding.   \n\nThe ECHO program focuses on\
  \ five key pediatric outcomes that have a high public health impact: prenatal, perinatal,\
  \ and postnatal outcomes; upper and lower airway conditions; obesity; neurodevelopment;\
  \ and positive child health or well-being."
