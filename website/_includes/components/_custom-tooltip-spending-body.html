For the purposes of this website, “Spending” is used as a general term to represent a specific value across various stages of the Federal government's fiscal and budget systems. The values used to calculate the total spending for a given fiscal year will vary based on the FPI's program type. For Federal assistance listings and Acquisition programs, spending is represented by the program's obligation amount. For tax expenditures, spending is represented by the sum of revenue loss and outlay amount. For interest on the public debt, spending is represented by the outlay amount. For more information, please refer to the FPI data, terms, & concepts page .
